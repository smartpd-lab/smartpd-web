SMARTPD LAB · V0.3

Take a guided eye-level photo

Use your phone's front camera and a standard-size plastic card. This prototype tests the photo-taking process only.

Research prototype: no pupillary distance is calculated. Do not use photos or this prototype to order prescription eyewear.

01Prepare your card

Use a flat plastic membership card the same size as a standard credit card (85.60 × 53.98 mm). Cover any sensitive information without covering its edges or corners.

Illustration: phone at eye level, card flat against the forehead, with all four card corners visible.

  1. Hold your phone in your left hand at approximately arm's length, with the camera at eye level.
  2. Hold the card at its right edge with your right hand. Place the back of the card flat against your forehead above your eyebrows.
  3. Keep the long edge horizontal, both eyes visible, and all four card corners unobstructed.

A short demonstration video is planned; this version provides an illustrated guide.

02Choose how to take the photo

RECOMMENDED

Phone selfie

On your computer, scan this QR code with your phone to open SmartPD over HTTPS. Follow the instructions on your computer while taking the photo on your phone.

smartpd-lab.github.io/smartpd-web/
ALTERNATIVE

Computer camera

You can also use a computer webcam. Sit facing the camera at eye level, keep the card against your forehead, and follow the same photo steps below.

Computer webcams may differ in distance and image quality; this is only a capture test.

03Camera and photo preview

Start the camera on the device you are using. Take a photo after the three-second countdown. The photo remains in this browser's temporary memory.
